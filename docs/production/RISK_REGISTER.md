# Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| CPI too high | Medium | High | Test creative before full build |
| D1 retention low | Medium | High | Improve tutorial and first 10 levels |
| Gameplay too shallow | High | High | Add shelter/hero meta early |
| Ads cause churn | Medium | High | Use rewarded-first ad strategy |
| Scope creep | High | High | Lock MVP scope and kill nonessential features |
| Economy inflation | Medium | Medium | Use remote config and staged rollout |
| Clone competition | High | Medium | Move fast with LiveOps and disaster hook |
