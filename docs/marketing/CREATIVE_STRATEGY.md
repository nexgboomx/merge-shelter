# Creative Strategy

## Main Ad Angles

### 1. Can You Survive 60 Seconds?
Hook:
- Zombies approach.
- Player makes a wrong merge.
- Shelter collapses.
- CTA: "Try to survive Night 7."

### 2. Satisfying Merge Defense
Hook:
- Merge metal into turret.
- Turret upgrades visually.
- Enemies melt.
- Strong sound/VFX.

### 3. Wrong Choice vs Right Choice
Hook:
- Split screen.
- Bad player builds food.
- Good player builds wall/turret.
- Viewers want to correct the mistake.

### 4. Base Evolution
Hook:
- Tiny tent to fortified bunker.
- Fast upgrade montage.
- Reward cadence visible.

### 5. Disaster Board
Hook:
- Blizzard freezes cells.
- Player must adapt quickly.
- Boss smashes row.

## Creative Production Rule
Every new feature should create at least 3 possible ad videos.
